# Step 5 — Ask your agent about your ads

← [Step 4](04-pull-your-ads.md) · Next: [Step 6 — Autopilot while you sleep](06-autopilot.md)

Open Claude Code (or any agent) in this folder and ask. It reads `ads.csv` and answers with the rows that prove it.

- Which ad spent the most last week, and what did it bring?
- Which ads spent money and brought zero messages? Is that enough spend to judge?
- Rank my ads by cost per message. Which 2 should I keep?
- Which ad has a good CTR but no results? What does that tell me?
- Write me a 5-line summary of yesterday's ads.

بالعربي:
- أنهي إعلان بيصرف ومش بيجيب رسايل؟
- رتّبلي الإعلانات من الأرخص للأغلى في تكلفة الرسالة.
- لو هقفل إعلان واحد النهارده، أقفل مين وليه؟

Tips for good answers:
- Compare ads on the result they were made for (a WhatsApp ad on messages, a form ad on leads).
- An ad with no results is only "bad" after it spent 2-3x what one result is worth to you.
- Meta's numbers are not sales. If you track customers in a sheet, give it to the agent too.

✅ You can stop here: your agent can read your ads. Step 6 is optional.
