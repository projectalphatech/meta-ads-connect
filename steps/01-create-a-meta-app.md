# Step 1 — Create a Meta app (5 min)

← [Back to the steps](../README.md) · Next: [Step 2 — Make a token](02-make-a-token.md)

An "app" is just your key holder. Nobody else sees it, and it needs no review to read your own ads.

1. Open **https://developers.facebook.com/apps** and log in with the Facebook account that runs your ads.
   First time? Click **Get started**, accept the terms, verify with your phone.
2. Click **Create app**.
3. Use case: pick the one about **ads / Marketing API**.
   Don't see it? Pick **Other**, then app type **Business**.
4. App name: anything, e.g. `my ads reader`. Contact email: yours.
5. If it asks for a **Business portfolio**: pick yours if you have one, or skip.
6. Inside the app, check that **Marketing API** is in the products list. If not: **Add product** → Marketing API → **Set up**.

✅ **Done when:** you see an **App ID** at the top of the app dashboard.

Meta renames buttons often. If a button is not where this says, look for the closest name, or ask your agent: "I see X, Y, Z on screen, what do I click?"
