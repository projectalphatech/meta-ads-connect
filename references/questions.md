# Questions to ask once connected

Paste any of these to your agent after `insights` made `ads.csv`. Works in Arabic too.

- Which ad spent the most last week, and what did it bring?
- Which ads spent money and brought zero leads / messages? Is it enough spend to judge yet?
- Rank my ads by cost per message (or per lead). Which 2 should I keep?
- Which ad has a good CTR but no results? What does that tell me?
- Compare this week to last week (run `insights --days 14` and split by date).
- Write me a 5-line WhatsApp summary of yesterday's ads.

بالعربي:
- أنهي إعلان بيصرف ومش بيجيب رسايل؟
- رتّبلي الإعلانات من الأرخص للأغلى في تكلفة الرسالة.
- لو هقفل إعلان واحد النهارده، أقفل مين ولية؟
